Privacy Policy — MAF VPS Backup

Last updated: November 2, 2026

MAF VPS Backup is a personal backup utility operated exclusively by its owner. This policy explains its intended Google user data practices.

Google Drive access

With the owner's explicit OAuth consent, the utility accesses the owner's Google Drive to upload selected backup files, list or verify the files created by the utility, and download those files for restoration. The intended configuration requests the narrowest practical Drive permissions, such as access to files created by the utility.

Data handling and storage

Only files expressly selected and authorized by the owner are backed up. Where encryption is enabled, files are encrypted locally before being transferred to the owner's Google Drive. Authentication credentials and encryption secrets are stored privately by the owner, not published on this website. No backup file contents are made publicly available by this website.

Data sharing

The utility is not offered to third parties. It does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers. Transfers to Google Drive are solely for the owner's authorized storage and recovery operations. It does not use Google user data to train AI models.

Retention and deletion

The owner controls the retention and deletion of files stored in their Google Drive. The owner may revoke the application's Google access from Google Account security settings and remove stored backup files separately. Revoking access does not automatically delete already-stored files.

Public website

This website is an informational static page. It has no sign-in, analytics, advertising, tracking scripts or user-data collection forms.

Contact

For questions about this personal utility, use the application support contact displayed in its Google OAuth consent screen.
