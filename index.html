MAF VPS Backup

Personal, owner-operated backup utility.

MAF VPS Backup is used by its owner to securely store and restore selected backup files in the owner's own Google Drive account. Its intended configuration uses rclone to encrypt the selected files on the owner's computer or server before transferring them to Google Drive.

This is not a publicly available backup service. It does not offer accounts or access to other users. Only the owner authorizes access to their Google account.

Google Drive access is requested solely for user-authorized backup, integrity checking and restoration. The intended least-privilege configuration operates on files created by this application, subject to the scopes selected during setup.
